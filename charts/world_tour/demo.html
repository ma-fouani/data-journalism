Pager Attack - 2024

On 17 September 2024 thousands of pagers carried by Hezbollah members (and others) detonated nearly simultaneously across Lebanon (and parts of Syria). A second wave on 18 September 2024 involved weaponised walkie-talkies. The attacks caused dozens of deaths and several thousand injuries, overwhelming hospitals and prompting calls for international investigation. Multiple reputable sources report that the operation was attributed to Israel by regional actors and many international media organizations.

Pager attack: unlike a single blast (Hiroshima or Beirut port), the pager attack was a distributed, coordinated detonation of many small devices across many locations. That makes an “area” comparison imprecise — the greater humanitarian impact came from number of simultaneous incidents and the high number of casualties and wounded treated across many hospitals.
